Frequently Asked Questions

How far in advance should I commission a wedding song? +

We recommend starting at least 3–4 weeks before your wedding date to allow time for your narrative session, composition, and any revisions. Rush timelines can sometimes be accommodated — message us on WhatsApp to check availability.

Can I hear the song before the wedding day? +

Yes. You'll receive your finished track ahead of your date so you can approve it, share it with your band or DJ if needed, and simply enjoy it before the big reveal.

Which package suits a first dance song? +

The Cinematic package (£120) is our most popular choice for weddings — a full 5-minute mastered composition with a picture slide video, ideal for a first dance or ceremony moment.

Can the song include multiple languages or cultural elements? +

Absolutely. We regularly compose songs that weave in Ghanaian, Afrobeats, gospel, or other cultural and language elements important to your story — just share this in your enquiry.
