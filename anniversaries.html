Custom Anniversary Songs That Relive Your Love Story

Relive your unique love story through masterfully produced music, arranged straight from the heart, for any anniversary milestone.

Every Year Deserves Its Own Verse

Whether it's your first year together or your fiftieth, a custom anniversary song captures what a card can't: the specific texture of your relationship, told back to you in music. We work from the memories, running jokes, and turning points you share with us to write lyrics that feel unmistakably like your story.

Many couples commission a new anniversary song every few years as a growing musical record of their marriage or partnership — a tradition that turns into its own kind of heirloom over time.
